Task identity
Reward Collectors is a value - location association task including two distinct roles: Active Navigator and Passive Observer
Environment
Participants navigate an augmented reality space in a real world laboratory environment with various visual landmarks that are similar in content to one another. 
Initial Encoding phase
Participants move to treasure chests placed at random locations. Each chest reveals a coin that is worth 1 of 3 values (0 points, 5 points, and 10 points), and participants are required to remember the location of each of these coins.
